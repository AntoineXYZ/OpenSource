Recovery validation
===================

The user builds manually; the assistant must never initiate a build. Native
harnesses are outside game targets and have not been built/run by the assistant.
Python references and synthetic fixtures do not establish C++ runtime or visual
parity. The user verified the viewer, cell editing and fresh designs; remaining
native/UI checks are below. Run commands from the repository root.

Manual client checklist (after the user's build)
-----------------------------------------------
Exercise standalone AuSceneClient -legacy-spacecraft [file] and the embedded
chooser. VS Code's "Legacy spacecraft viewer (already built)" profiles do not
build. Paths must match the executable platform. Compare the three sample ships
and controlled fixtures with the original client, including saved-file reloads.

Panel, editing and saving:
- F6: right dock, float/redock/close/reopen, design-disk title icon, dark controls,
  rounded palette groups, icons without legacy DLLs. No menu bar, bottom status
  bar, cursor readout, Fit button, canvas scrollbars or top summary strip.
  Ctrl+N/O/S, F/F6 and deck-view Escape work with the panel hidden/floating;
  Escape in a dialog dismisses it. Disabled/building-only tools remain absent
  or inert. Room Number wraps 1..256; minimum TL displays 32..1.
- Grid: finite 80x80 cells (+/-226.27416996ft), brighter ten-cell lines, four
  directions and correctly positioned compass labels. Fit includes labels;
  zoom preserves label size, middle-drag pans. Room labels use first-boundary
  anchors, subtype+1, original colors/shadows; edits/deck switches leave no
  stale labels, and clicks pass through labels.
- Deck navigation stops at -41/+41; new rooms at -28/+28. Erasure works outside
  room limits. On deck 29, hatch/top-turret attachment to deck 28 succeeds while
  room painting fails without removing it. Test both render modes.
- New defaults: streamline on, armor 0, TL1. Loading preserves settings without
  dirtying. Test Save/Discard/Cancel on New/Open/Exit, failed-load preservation,
  last-directory fallback, always-prompt Save, suffix/overwrite handling and
  failed/cancelled saves retaining edits. Reopen copies; repeated unchanged saves
  must match. Open the current dirty file after saving it to the same path.
- Fresh-process IDs: blank 20000, first import 20001, next New/import 20002;
  cancelled/failed actions and saves consume none. Header/rooms/equipment agree.
  Match import sequences when comparing old-client IDs; architect may differ.
- Paint/repaint/erase adjacency, containment, disjoint/corner touches, bridges,
  holes, closed loops, diagonal/negative seams and XY +/-120/deck +/-28 limits.
  Compare boundaries, IDs and equipment changes, not just appearance.
- Place each enabled equipment type in allowed/disallowed rooms; check bridge
  exceptions, single stations, rotations, negative decks, vehicle footprints
  near edges/at 45 degrees, and brush sizes 1/26. Repaint reconciles equipment;
  area erase is type-specific. Dragging within one snapped point edits once.
- Save transporter pads across room numbers/decks: one module per group, none
  for consoles alone; removing final pads/bays reconciles stale/default maps.
  Embedded exit restores music; reopen Legacy then Modern via the chooser.

Ship statistics and the single F6 information pane:
- Empty designs show tips; hulls show stats without tabs. Match Star Driver's
  compact pale text/navy background, uppercase headings, rules, nested 10px
  indentation and wrapped lines. Yellow Modified appears on edit, clears on
  save. Updates preserve scrolling; New/Open reset it. Feedback is at the end
  and in the tooltip. Section order: HULL, ENGINEERING, FUEL, AVIONICS, ARMAMENT,
  CREW, QUARTERS, HOLD, VEHICLE BAYS, DAMAGE CONTROL, MANUFACTURE.
- Room/equipment/header edits refresh stats. A 32ft2 floor gives 256ft3; double
  berths give two crew/officer/passenger places in the corresponding room.
  Galley/lounge affect passenger class. Sensor/shield stations count as both
  console and system. Weapon bays group across decks; transporters and their
  station checks use deck plus room number (as do surgery station checks).
- TL changes capacity/capacitor/performance, not reactor output or fueled mass;
  armor/streamline affect mass, acceleration and HP. Rocket drives omit holding
  endurance; energy/harvester bays distinguish capacitor shots from output.
  Save/render toggles preserve stats and dirty state. Unrecovered manufacture
  TL/cost, absorption and ammunition damage must remain explicitly unavailable.

Rendering, walkthrough and audio:
- Render Mode keeps its original palette icon/position. Repeated toggles retain
  floor-plan camera/tool and avatar position/facing. F does not fit the 3D view;
  Home finds clear standing space or reports failure. New/Open reset avatar and
  doors; geometry edits retain unchanged-door states. Deck selection moves the
  outside studio plane without teleporting the avatar.
- Compare armor 0..10, glass floors/tops/windows, partitions, fuel rooms,
  negative decks, open-below/gravity wells, stairs, door/hatch openings, turrets
  and equipment. Console/vehicle textures need no legacy DLLs; effects advance
  every 100ms without moving geometry. Parked vehicles remain static; logical
  markers have plan icons without invented preview models.
- WASD/mouse, Shift run, Q forward, Space jump, C/Z flight/wells, E ladder entry/
  exit. Nearby ladders alone must not suspend gravity; include deck-29 hatches.
  On ladders W follows head pitch (up/down), S reverses it; level W adds no
  climbing force. C/Z remain pitch-independent. Shift does not boost climbing.
  Ship interiors restore gravity; studio gravity lands feet on the selected
  plane. Check walls/slopes/corners/stairs and open hatches for tunnelling.
  Consoles/berths/chairs/vehicles/turret guns are passable; machinery/tables block.
- Return/right-click toggles hand mode; Ctrl releases temporarily. Focus loss,
  dialogs, floating panels and application switches clear held input and avoid
  trapped cursors/stale mouse deltas. Ctrl+S/F6 still work. Crosshair appears on
  empty designs; hull targeting adds name/icon/health bar and decays on look-away.
  Aim at consoles, machinery, pads and turrets: subsystem icons/HP must switch;
  sensor/shield consoles show command/control. Test shared batches, occlusion,
  console swivel and deletion refresh. See WALKTHROUGH-SUBSYSTEM-TARGETS.txt.
- Automatic doors/hatches animate closed/half/open; idle floor support must not
  open a hatch. Ctrl+P/C within six feet works on current/above-deck doors; hand
  clicks toggle nearby panels. Hull/large doors stay manual; open doors persist.
  Test rapid reversal, both texture sides and stacked doors without joining
  unrelated neighbors. Walking/gravity/door operation must not change saved bytes.
- With sfx.har, test door families, shared stacked-door channels, metal/interior
  and hard-floor/outside footsteps, run pitch and loop stops. Respect sound
  settings, reuse game OpenAL services, and avoid leaks on exit/reopen/app close.
- At helm/engineering, G before P must not initialize a fresh design. P then G
  shows one blue Intercom welcome with the design name. Holding P/G must not
  repeat toggles; later power cycles, seat changes, edits and render toggles
  must not repeat the welcome. New/Open permits it again. Check configured
  bindings and H full/hold (stations cannot hold), absent plant/drive units,
  message fading and channel/HUD filters. Controls must not operate on foot.
  Plant/drive/sensor/shield/FTL/transporter/weapon effects remain off with empty
  fuel/charge; berth/life-support/surgery effects require life-support units.
  Repeat with hand-cursor clicks on the two console buttons. Check indicator
  artwork, maneuver Off/On/Hold cycling, drag cancellation, blank/lower-panel
  clicks, occlusion and changed camera/screen angles. Clicks must not also
  operate doors behind the console. See WALKTHROUGH-SYSTEM-STARTUP.txt.
