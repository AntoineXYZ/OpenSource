2016 spacecraft statistics: aggregation evidence
===============================================

Primary evidence was recovered read-only through the Ghidra MCP bridge on
2026-10-05, explicitly targeting the 64-bit /AuAtom13.dll and /AuData13.dll.
The new aggregation and performance models do not execute the old binaries.

Core analysis
-------------

AuAtom13.dll 1803c63c0 DaSpaceShip::AnalyzeDesign(bool) constructs a fresh
DqDesignAnalysis at DaSpaceShip+0x778 and walks room/detail records. Its complete
decompilation, together with IsHullContiguous at 1803f9780, is preserved in
pseudocode/AuAtom13.dll_DesignStatsAggregation.txt.

Each room contributes round(abs(polygon area)*8) cubic feet independently; the
sum is not a union of overlapping polygons. The fixed deck height is 8 feet.
The following DqDesignAnalysis offsets identify the named volume totals:

  +00c Bridge        room type 2
  +010 Brig          room type 3
  +030 Engine Room   room type 8
  +050 Vehicle Bay   room type 13
  +054 Fuel Cell     room type 9
  +0b8 Hold          room type 5
  +1a4 Sensor Room   room type 16
  +1ac Shield Room   room type 17
  +228 Hull          all rooms
  +22c Weapon Bay    room type 21

Galley type10 sets +065; Lounge type14 sets +067. These are presence flags,
independent of equipment. Weapon bay records are keyed only by room subtype
(the zero-based displayed room number), so matching numbers on different
decks share their weapon system. The new model retains volumes for every room
type as well as the original special totals.

Ship extents omit room types22..24 (building-only rooms), but their volumes
were not excluded in the original aggregation. X/Y extrema come from room
boundaries. The original expands Z min/max by minus/plus8 feet, resulting in
height=(maximumDeck-minimumDeck+2)*8. DaSpaceShip::IsHullContiguous starts with
the first room and repeatedly joins same-deck or adjacent-deck rooms when a
vertex is inside/on the other boundary, testing both directions. Point-only
contacts count; crossing edges with no contained vertices do not. The new
implementation reuses surviving AuPointArray Area, PointInBoundary and
PointOnBoundary_2D operations. Empty inspection-only records are skipped,
matching the original stream array reader which discarded empty rooms.

Equipment relationships
-----------------------

The entire 143-entry recovered GD table supplies equipment mass, minimum TL
and original crew-table values. Source: detail-types.json and DnDetailType::Rx.
AnalyzeDesign adds int(float(massKg)*2.2046225f) pounds PER detail, rather than
converting a combined kilogram total. Hidden flags do not exclude equipment.
For vehicle locations57..64 it adds int(DaVehicle::Mass(CID)) independently.
AuAtom13.dll 1800f70b0 maps CIDs to the following truncated pounds:

  6 -> 5511; 7,8,77 -> 22046; 9 -> 55115; 28,82 -> 6400;
  104 -> 66138; 132 -> 320; 133 -> 230; 231 -> 26455; other -> 0.

Old fighter-marker detail types41..48 are NOT vehicle locations in this binary:
DnDetailType::IsVehicleLoc at AuData13.dll180034720 accepts only57..64, and
AnalyzeDesign has no specific cases for41..48. They retain their zero mass.

Room assignment uses the first matching room ID, not geometric containment.
Berths (types2,25..31 double;65..72 single) contribute only in room types:
  1 Barracks -> troop; 4 Officer Cabin -> officer; 7 Crew Cabin -> crew;
  19 Stateroom -> passenger. DnDetail::Bunks is at AuData13.dll18002c530.

Machinery counters increment for every matching detail, even without a room:
  maneuver10; power plant14; sensors15+16; shields17+18; weapons23; FTL24;
  life support32; transporter pad20. Sensor/Shield stations15/17 therefore
count both as a console and as one system unit. Console list types are
1,4,6,7,12,13,15,17,19,73 and require a matching room. Helm12 also sets a
presence flag before room lookup. Surgery11, turret21,22,33..40 and game
table74/75 lists require a matching room. Troop station141 does not.

Transporter pads are grouped by (uint8(deck)<<8)|roomSubtype; distinct room
IDs with the same number on the same deck share their transporter system.
Weapon units23 and emitters99 join their room's number across all decks.
An empty weapon bay is still represented. Vehicles57..64 contribute one
parking location and one commodity complement entry per detail.

AuOpening::IsDoor at AuData13.dll180073a50 recognizes detail types3,5,8,
49..54,77..97. Hull openings/entrances are3,9,50,52,91..97, confirmed by
DnDetailType::IsHullOpening180032640 and AuOpening::IsHullOpening180073e30.
The original machinery system permits boarding through either such an
opening or a transporter pad. Implicit floor openings are not hull entrances.

Crew and passenger classification
---------------------------------

DqDesignAnalysis::Report assembly1801c83ee..1801c854a counts captain's chairs73
as officer stations and all other registered consoles as crew stations. This
is different from summing the GD crew column (captain's chairs have crew=0).
Assembly1801ce810..1801ce834 counts pilots for parked CIDs6..9 or231 and drivers
for all other parked CIDs. Turret gunners equal turret count; guards equal
troop stations. The new crewRequired is the sum of these six role counts.

IsFirstClass180073bf0 requires passenger berths, Galley and Lounge.
IsSecondClass180074500 requires passenger berths and Galley. The report assigns
all passenger berths to first, second or third class in that priority order.

Equipment TL is the maximum GD minimum-tech value, resolving equal nonzero
values to the lowest detail type. AnalyzeDesign also considers
max(0,crewBerths+passengerBerths+troopBerths-8), clamps it to255, and attributes
it to berth type2; officer berths do not enter this occupancy constraint.

Known scope boundaries
----------------------

The complete original analysis also constructs door spans, implicit floor
openings, routing/collision geometry, manufacturing requirements and room/
equipment interaction locations. Those structures are not duplicated by the
numeric aggregation. Door counts reflect authored door/hatch details; malformed
or overlapping imported details can differ from the original generated-opening
lists. Existing authoring reconciliation handles normal valid designs.

DeManufacturingProcess::InitSpacecraft180126fb0 raises process TL for certain
required materials after collecting room/detail ListComponents. This separate
path is captured in pseudocode/AuData13.dll_180126fb0_InitSpacecraft.txt; the
numeric model does not claim a recovered full manufacturing bill of materials.
The process starts from the design's current calculated TL and can raise it
for material CIDs268->21,227->3,264->9,265->18,228->6,271->23,146->9,145->18,
266->12,269->23. Verify material dependencies before adding these to a model.

DaSpaceShip::DesignSave1803e5710 ensures analysis is current, then calls
DnSpaceShip::ReconcileModules18017e0c0. That function adds/trims bay/transporter
module choices; it does not overwrite saved hull volume/structure/tech fields.
No speculative save-header mutation was introduced by the aggregation work.
