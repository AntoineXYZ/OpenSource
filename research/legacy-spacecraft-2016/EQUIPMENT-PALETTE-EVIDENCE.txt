Equipment palette and hull integration, 2016
==========================================

The existing palette coordinates, icons and Render Mode control are retained.
Rounded red frames group room tools, stations/machinery, openings, stairs,
vehicles, markers and brush/erase controls without moving the Render Mode button.
The palette enables a tool only when its procedural or imported resource model
is implemented (or it is a recovered logical marker with no physical model).
All 51 equipment palette slots are enabled, including the six vehicles.

Original EXE Qt slots and constructors were traced using the existing read-only
IDA baseline and Ghidra MCP. Slot bodies are captured in pseudocode/
equipment-palette-slots.txt. The constructor dtype/CID arguments define the
catalogue in aulegacyshipviewer.cpp; vehicles use57..64 and commodities9,8,231,
104,133,82 in the original six button locations.

DdDesignDetail constructor140311230 and handlers1403113a0/140311520 confirm
left-click and left-drag placement. Only types10,14,16,18,23,24,32 request the
brush radius; erase-one requests0 and erase-area requests the current radius.
AuView brush handlers1402e8cb0/1402e8c90 clamp its stored radius to0..25.
AuTabDesign::TimerUpdate1402d33e0 displays radius+1, i.e.1..26.

DdDesignBerth140310560 and DdDesignVehicleLoc140319220 start orientation0.
Their shared key handler1403107e0 maps Qt Left0x1000012 to incrementing the
orientation index and Right0x1000014 to decrementing, wrapping0..7. The berth
arrays are65..72 for single and2,25..31 for double; vehicles use57..64.
Placement itself does not rotate an existing item on repeated clicks.
The offline tool preview snaps with round-away-from-zero like EncodeGridCoord.
Vehicle outlines use the original half-extents and the selected orientation.
Floor-plan record markers use the original palette icons; they are inspection
icons, not a claim of exact original RenderDesign wireframe reproduction.
Dragging repeats an edit only after entering a different snapped point; a new
click resets the gesture. This prevents sub-grid motion from repeatedly erasing
co-located logical and physical equipment. Tool/deck changes also reset it.

Turret hull geometry in aulegacyshipmesh.cpp
------------------------------------------
RenderTurret18044dca0 supplies13 vertices and16 triangles, with ShipTexture56.
The unrotated canopy is an octagonal half-dome facing+Y. RenderExteriorTops
18042a140 rotates it+pi/2 about X for type22; RenderExteriorBottoms1804274c0
rotates it-pi/2 for type33. The detail lies on the authored deck plane; the
type22 roof belongs to the deck below. Surface polygons are clipped to the
octagonal opening and both interior and exterior canopy faces are emitted.

RenderExterior180426360 splits side-turret spans at location+/-2.8284271245
along the wall. Assembly uses XMM9 loaded from180795818 (double bits
4006a09e6676c64a). The resulting wall tag31 selects DnRoom::WallProfile31:
(offset0,z0,strip armor>3?4:5), (1.5,4-sqrt8,3), (1.5,4+sqrt8,5), (0,8,0).
RenderExteriorWalls18042cdb0 emits the same13-point canopy for strip3,
rotated with the wall and centered on the strip, plus the surrounding corner
triangles. ShipTexture56 covers both the dome and its corner frame. The port
cuts the octagonal opening from that strip instead of retaining a wall behind
the canopy. Armor-dependent strips and wall end caps continue around it.
The frame retains the original eight corner triangles and their distinct UVs,
including the .6767767 diagonal coordinates; wider spans add two side triangles.

Standalone resource bootstrap includes AuAtomRes, AuModelRes and AuTexture,
so consoles, model materials and machinery use the same packaged resources as
the embedded viewer. No legacy DLLs are loaded at runtime.

No build, compiler, native harness or rendered visual comparison was run.
Manual checks and remaining limitations are recorded in TESTING.txt and the
equipment/stair/resource rendering evidence files.
