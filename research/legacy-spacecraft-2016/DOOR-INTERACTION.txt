2016 spacecraft designer: doors and hatches
Recovered 2026-10-04 from the original 2016 Ghidra instance.

Rendering and animation
-----------------------
AuAtom13.dll DaSpaceShip::AnimateDeviceObjects, 0x1803c9950, updates
device animation at most once every 100 ms. It selects three atlas regions
for doors: closed, half-open and open. When the logical door state changes,
the next device tick selects the half-open image; the following tick selects
the destination image. Reversing during the half-open image selects the new
destination on the next tick. There is no moving leaf geometry in this path.

The original ShipTexture families and their three consecutive indices are:
  large interior 2/3/4; large crew 5/6/7; large hull 8/9/10;
  crew door 11/12/13; crew hatch 14/15/16;
  hull door 19/20/21; hull hatch 22/23/24;
  interior door 30/31/32; interior hatch 34/35/36.
Each sequence is closed / half-open / open. The existing embedded 2016
spacecraft atlas already contains every image, including the alpha channels.
RenderHatch 0x18042ee90 starts with the half-open region and assigns a
per-door UV-offset animation index. RenderInterior 0x18042f410 and
RenderExteriorWalls 0x18042cdb0 use the same mechanism for wall panels.
Hull doors have different panels on their two sides: the interior uses the
crew-door family, or large crew-door family for a large hull door. The small
interior panel spans the full eight-foot deck height; the exterior hull-door
panel is seven feet high beneath its projecting upper hull profile. Both
sides share the same logical door and animation state.

Hatches are flat 4 x 4 foot quads, axis-aligned or rotated 45 degrees by the
cell orientation. A diamond therefore extends 2.8284271245 feet from its
center. Detail rotation affects the ladder, not the panel's texture frame.
The uppermost room deck is 28, but a ceiling hatch may be on boundary deck
29. Its ladder extends downward from that boundary through room deck 28.
AppendEquipment permits this additional boundary deck only for hatch ladders;
other equipment remains limited to room decks, and hatch deck 30 is excluded.
Wall openings and railings have no open/close animation. Neither do turret
apertures. Logical door state changes independently of visual frame timing.

Interaction and designer differences
-----------------------------------
DaSpaceShip::ClosestClosedDoor 0x1803d1880 and ClosestOpenDoor 0x1803d2070
consider openings on the actor's deck or the deck immediately above. The
horizontal squared distance must be <= 36 (six feet). Wall distances are to
the closest point on the opening segment; hatch distances are to the hatch
center. AuData13.dll AuOpening::Distance2_2D, 0x180072720, confirms that
distinction. Equal-distance candidates retain the first opening in order.
AnalyzeDesign uses center +/- 2 feet for a small wall door's operational
segment (the drawn panel extends +/- 2.5 feet). Large door operational
segments use an 11/12-foot inset at each wall end. These dimensions also
apply to the nearest-door query, independently of the drawn panel edges.
Crew/hull locks and crew privileges are world state, not saved design fields.

Original executable tooltips confirm Ctrl+P for Open Door and Ctrl+C for
Close Door. The cursor-release tooltip explicitly describes using the
avatar's hand to open doors. These are the original actions, not an E-key
binding. CloseDoor does not refuse a close because an actor occupies the
opening; its implementation directly changes the door state(s).

Small regular and crew doors/hatches open automatically when encountered
by movement. AuData13.dll AuPath::TestSpacecraftPath, 0x180107210, collects
eligible closed automatic doors and hatches; AuAtom13.dll
DaSpaceShip::IsPathBlocked, 0x1803fa2a0, subsequently calls OpenDoor on them.
The automatic types are exactly 5, 8, 49, 51, and 77 through 90, verified by
AuOpening::IsAutomaticDoor, 0x1800737e0. Large doors and hull doors/hatches
require manual operation. An automatic hatch in the cell above can also
be opened by the movement test. This evidence concerns movement interaction,
not the separate designer path checking tool.

World automatic closing is deliberately NOT used in the designer.
DaSpaceShip::Tock, 0x18046d6e0, returns immediately when InDesignEnvironment
is true, before its call to CloseAutomaticDoors at 0x18046e12f. The world
closing routine 0x1803d0640 would otherwise close automatic doors when no
eligible person remains within three feet horizontally, subject to deck and
pose checks. Applying that world rule in the editor would change 2016
designer behavior.

Stacked large doors
-------------------
AuOpening::CanOperateStack, AuData13.dll 0x180071f80, returns true only for
large hull (3), large interior (53) and large crew (54) doors.
DaSpaceShip::OpenDoor 0x18040a1a0 and CloseDoor 0x1803d0c60 operate contiguous
vertical stacks at the same authored detail x/y, including mixed large-door
families. They scan upward while each next opening is a stackable type with
primary room type 11 or 15 and adjacent room type 0, 11 or 15. They scan
downward only while the current opening satisfies that void-room condition,
including the bottom stackable opening before testing its room condition.
A missing opening, a nonstackable opening, or a disallowed room pairing
ends the relevant scan. Merely sharing a room number or being nearby is not
enough to link doors. The saved design is not changed by opening a door.
AnalyzeDesign stores that authored detail location at AuOpening +0x40,
independently of the rendered opening endpoints. AuOpening::BaseCenter,
0x180071c90, computes the midpoint for large doors, but stack matching does
not call BaseCenter. Different opening widths can therefore still belong
to the same stack when the detail markers are aligned.

Implementation
--------------
aulegacyshipequipment.h/.cpp exposes the recovered three-frame state update,
material selection, classifications, six-foot nearest-door query, and stack
group selection. DoorDefinitions associates each rendered panel with its
detail ID, derives wall endpoints from the tagged panel mesh with the above
operational endpoint adjustments, and resolves
adjacent room types against the authored boundaries. Hidden detail bytes do
not suppress these structural door/hatch panels, matching the earlier
detail-hidden-surface-evidence capture. Definitions without a rendered panel
are excluded. Renderer state and collision are separate consumers of these
helpers; neither changes the .ship writer or serializes open states.
