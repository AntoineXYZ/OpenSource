Procedural equipment rendering, 2016 client
==========================================

Source: read-only Ghidra AuAtom13.dll decompiles captured under pseudocode/.
Implementation: src/lib/designer/legacy/aulegacyshipequipment.{h,cpp}.
This file records reconstruction evidence; it is not original source.

Dispatch and placement
----------------------
RenderInterior 18042f410, final detail loop at 18043290a, suppresses equipment
when hiddenRaw is nonzero. Its structural wall/hatch consumers do not suppress
hidden details. Each procedural model uses the detail's authored feet position
and deck*8; berth and ladder variants use DnDetailType::Rotation narrowed to a
float angle before their transform. Equipment batches remain visible above the
four-foot cutaway-wall plane on the selected deck. Ladders belong to the deck
below their hatch because their geometry extends down eight feet.

Animation
---------
AnimateDeviceObjects 1803c9950 advances the effect atlas every100ms. Batches
retain numeric original EffectTexture indexes and exact series lengths:
berth0/4, life8/4, gravity16/4, power20/4, sensor24/3, shield27/4,
surgery40/3, idle transporter47/4, weapon51/4, FTL55/4.
These are texture animations. No arbitrary mechanical rotation is introduced.
The renderer owns the recovered effect atlas and frame rectangle table.
The designer has no live ship systems: transporter uses its idle series47,
rather than active transport series43; weapons likewise use the preview series.

Structural railing56 is implemented in aulegacyshipmesh.cpp's partition pass.
RenderInterior 180430ca3 multiplies the boundary unit vector by .1 (constant
180748b58); end posts49 run z0..2.7. Top rail48 runs z2.7..3 and spans the whole
simplified wall run. Its .3 height constant is at1807bbd48. Each original room
boundary contributes its facing side, like the other interior partitions.

Stairs101..140 and turret housings are independently recovered in the stairs
helper and main mesh respectively. Stairs do not automatically cut floor holes:
the original plane routines only test hatch and turret details; authors use
open-below rooms for stairwells. Resource-backed consoles, table/chairs and
vehicles use the separate aulegacyshipvehicles importer and original assets.

Logical markers76/98/99 and troop station141 deliberately have no 3D model.
RenderInteriorNoModel18043aac5 loads mask0x600000f00ff and tests(type-57) at
18043acba; this skips76/98/99 along with the separately rendered resources.
An explicit comparison against141 at18043acd3 skips the troop station too.
Neither RenderInterior nor RenderEnvironmentObject has a model case for these
four types. They remain usable authoring records without invented geometry.
The assembly evidence is saved as180439ac0_marker_skip_evidence.txt. Flagpole100
is not in that skip set and remains unsupported. Unsupported visible imported
details produce a type-specific warning; hidden details do not produce warnings.

Turret guns are separate resource geometry. CreateDeviceObjects1803dda11 selects
commodity102 (HeavyWeapon); initstatic.sql maps that commodity to mTurretGun.3dsN.
RenderGun18042ed70 applies only translation and orientation, without an override
texture. RenderEnvironmentObject's turret switch places top22 at z-model.minZ,
bottom33 at z-model.maxZ, both with90-degree yaw. Side mounts are two feet away
from their boundary anchors and centered at deckheight+4 (diagonal offsets use
the original1.414 value). The resource importer owns this model dispatch.
