2016 spacecraft stairs
=======================

Read-only Ghidra captures, all from AuAtom13.dll:
  RenderStairsStraight   0x1804466b0
  RenderStairsTurnLeft   0x180447020
  RenderStairsTurnRight  0x180448990
  RenderStairsUTurnLeft  0x180449780
  RenderStairsUTurnRight 0x18044a570

The new aulegacyshipstairs.cpp appends the recovered five stair families to
the mesh. Types 101..140 comprise five consecutive groups of eight angles.
DnDetailType::Rotation returns a float angle, promoted to double for the
original point transform. Odd orientations select the diagonal dimensions.
The final RenderInterior furniture loop skips details with hidden != 0.

Common geometry:
  StairCasing material 59, StairRiser 60, StairTread 61.
  Edge inset: 1/96 foot, named e below.
  Total rise: 8-e = 7.989583333333333 feet.
  Each step: one riser quad, one tread quad, two triangular side faces.
  The underside is a sloping casing quad, not a solid rectangular stair box.
  Turns have a six-face landing slab at half the total rise. Its underside
  sits one half-flight riser below its top. Landing sides use Riser, the
  upper face Tread, and the lower face Casing.

Straight:
  ceil(12.370968122512082) = 13 steps.
  Axis length 11.313708498, width 5.656854249 feet.
  Diagonal length 12, width 4 feet.
  Width is inset e at both outer edges. Positive local X climbs the stair.
  Result: 240 vertices (6 casing, 156 riser/sides, 78 tread).

Quarter-turn left/right:
  Two flights of ceil(6.185484061256041) = 7 steps each.
  Axis flight length and width both 5.656854249 feet.
  Diagonal flight length 8, width 4 feet.
  The first flight climbs positive X; the second climbs positive Y for a
  left turn or negative Y for a right turn. The landing spans local X from
  flight length to flight length+width-e. Flights inset e at side edges.
  Result: 300 vertices (18 casing, 192 riser/sides, 90 tread).

U-turn left/right:
  Two flights of 7 steps each; the second climbs negative local X.
  Axis flight length, width and landing depth all 5.656854249 feet.
  Diagonal flight length 8, width 6, landing depth 4 feet.
  The landing spans the combined two-flight width, with e at the outer
  edges. The shared center edge has no inset. Overall diagonal footprint
  is 12x12 feet before the outer edge inset.
  Result: 300 vertices with the same material counts as quarter-turns.

Riser and tread coordinates use full individual atlas rectangles for every
step. Side triangles use the corresponding triangular half of the riser
rectangle. Right-handed variants retain the original flight point order,
which matters for UV orientation. Casing normals follow the original fixed
45-degree slope normal; face winding is made consistent with that normal.

Floor openings are authored separately. RenderInteriorFloors/Ceilings test
IsAnyHatch and turret detail types 22/33 for apertures; they do not query
stairs. Stair IsValid checks its same-deck footprint only. Consequently the
mesh retains authored open-below rooms and does not invent automatic stair
holes or require an upper room that the original placement rules did not.

The independent manual native harness tests/native_ship_stairs.cpp checks
original vertex/material counts, cardinal bounds, rise, all 40 rotations,
visibility and winding. It has not been built or run by the assistant.
