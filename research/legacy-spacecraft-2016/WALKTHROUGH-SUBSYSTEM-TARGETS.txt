Maneuver, life support, reactor, sensor, shield and FTL use installed unit counts.
Transporters use pad count for the room key; weapons use units in that bay.

one for a nonempty volume. HullHits (AuData13.dll 0x1800733a0) subtracts those
three separately rounded values from total structure hits. Authoring remaining
hits equal maximum hits: no damage state is invented or serialized. Defensive
handling clamps a negative hull remainder and zero-capacity bar to zero.
