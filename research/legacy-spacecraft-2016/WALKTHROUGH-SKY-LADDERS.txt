2016 designer skybox and ladder attachment
==========================================
Recovered 2026-10-05 through read-only Ghidra MCP against the 2016 client.
No original executable was run or modified. No build was initiated.

Skybox evidence
---------------
Shores of Hazeron.exe 0x140244760 creates a temporary galaxy (enum 15,
ShoresOfHazeron), sector, solar system and homeworld for the offline studio.
The surrounding environment is generated, not serialized in the ship file.

AuAtom13.dll:
  0x1802fb170 DaDesignEnvironment::RenderEnvironment checks render-solid and
              delegates environment rendering through the parent system.
  0x180368c50 DaSector::RenderEnvironment creates auNebula and its skybox.
              A neighboring nebula contributes 1,048,576 particles when
              present. Galactic stars are sampled at offsets -500..500 in
              steps of five sectors. Density is sampled at (sector+offset)*10,
              multiplied by 100, divided by distance*.125 except at zero,
              and truncated to an integer star count. Star positions use
              offset*.2 plus independent random components in [-1,1].
  0x180182210 auNebula::CreateSkyBox renders six framebuffer faces into a
              cube texture using generated stars and nebula particles.
              It references :data/smoketransparent.png and txAsteroids.png.
  0x180183cb0 auNebula::RenderSkyBox draws the resulting cube texture.

There is no complete skybox PNG in this rendering path. Read-only decoding of
AuAtomRes13.dll Qt registrations 0x1030 and 0x10b0 confirms that the original
smoketransparent.png (64x64), txAsteroids.png (256x256), and ShoresOfHazeron.png
(1488x1200) have identical decoded pixels to the modern resources. PNG file
bytes differ due to encoding/metadata. No extracted override is needed.

Implementation uses the surviving auNebula generator, native skybox shader,
and AuDesignerView's existing AuAtomRenderInfo/AuGLEffects. Galactic sampling
is shared with DaSector::RenderEnvironment through
auNebula::CreateGalacticStarField. This is the existing in-game loop moved
into a common method, retaining its density, distance attenuation, positional
jitter, iteration order and random-number sequence for the same inputs.
The backdrop uses a local seed of 2016 and a sampled inhabited-density sector
in ShoresOfHazeron, without a nearby nebula. This repeatable preview
substitutes for the old random temporary world; it does not reproduce a
particular original session's sky or the player's live location. The modern
generator contains later star-sprite changes, so mixing the old star sample
distribution with it was inappropriate. Pixel-exact 2016 parity is not claimed.

The sky is rendered before geometry with rotation-only view transformation,
the configured FOV, and no depth writes. DrawSkyBox, DesignerShowSkyBox,
SkyBoxPaneSize and SkyBoxMipMap are respected. Resolution/mipmap changes
recreate the cube texture in a worker context; generation never changes the
QOpenGLWidget framebuffer or viewport. Context destruction cancels pending work.
Floor-plan mode does not render the sky. No world data or seed enters .ship.
The previous forced nearby nebula and 15% brightness multiplier were removed
following visual feedback. The game's non-nebula background renders at its
normal intensity, preserving star contrast against deep space. Ship lighting
is unaffected. The custom top-left deck/gravity and missing-texture overlays
are removed; texture warnings remain available through Warnings().

Preparation and render-mode entry
---------------------------------
The initial implementation ran all galaxy sampling and all six cube faces
inside the first walkthrough paint. For this galaxy the native sample grid
is 501*501*51 (12,801,051 samples), before star vertices and texture uploads.
That synchronous path blocks Qt event processing while it runs.

PrepareSky starts a low-priority QThread during floor-plan or walkthrough
painting. The worker owns its galaxy, dice, auNebula, shader programs, VAOs
and shared OpenGL context. All galaxy sampling, shader compilation, cube-face
allocation/drawing, mipmap generation and GPU completion run there. Sampling
checks cancellation between X slices and rendering checks between faces.
The seed, sample order, density and native face renderer are unchanged.

The main thread creates a six-face 1x1 navy-blue (#000080) placeholder cube.
It also creates Qt's offscreen surface and establishes context sharing before
transferring the context to the worker. Those small setup operations remain
on the GUI thread. Painting only polls completion with a nonblocking lock;
the finished shared texture replaces the placeholder without readback/upload,
a GPU fence wait or a thread join. Driver-level GPU contention is still possible.

Only texture ownership crosses threads; VAOs remain context-local. The worker
initializes only its three required shaders, with thread-local shader-source
scratch storage, and explicitly releases its shader programs and sprite
textures while its context is current. It finishes GPU rendering before
publishing the cube. Existing AuAtomRenderInfo skybox callers retain their
viewport restoration; the worker uses a widget-independent overload.

Changing resolution/mipmaps or destroying the widget context cancels the old
job and releases any unclaimed result. A new job deterministically resamples
the galaxy. Late results cannot replace a newer cube. Closing never joins a
worker; each worker disposes its context, then schedules its surface and
thread object for GUI-thread deletion. Unsupported threaded/shared GL or a
generation failure leaves the navy cube and a warning, without retrying each
paint. Completed cubes survive floor-plan/walkthrough mode switches.

Ladder evidence
---------------
AuAtom13.dll:
  0x1803cf440 DaSpaceShip::CanGrabLadder chooses a non-wall opening on the
              avatar's deck or the next deck, within a strict six-foot XY
              radius. The attachment XY is the hatch center; Z is retained.
              The path to that point must be clear.
  0x1802e9de0 DaCreature::GrabLadder(ship) translates to that point, rotates
              the avatar by detail orientation minus pi/2, and selects pose
              0x4a. An eye-position path check can reject and restore the old
              pose/transform. Successful attachment resets movement.
  0x1802e9720 DaCreature::FilterFinalMoveVector zeros X and Y for ladder
              poses 0x4a/0x4b, leaving only Z movement.

The human's native forward is +Y. With the recovered body rotation, the
legacy camera's initial azimuth is the hatch detail rotation (45-degree
steps). Rungs lie at local x=1.834 relative to the hatch center. The center
therefore places the avatar in front of the ladder rather than inside it.

Implementation obtains axes from authored hatch records, not mesh triangle
proximity. It selects the nearest reachable center within six feet, sweeps
the standing capsule to it, snaps XY and camera facing once, zeros velocity,
and clears continuous forward movement. Subsequent look input is unrestricted.
Attached movement passes configured MoveForward/MoveBackward (W/S by default)
unchanged to DaPerson: head pitch determines the vertical component. W climbs
up looking up and down looking down; S reverses it. Level forward input adds
no climbing force. MoveUp/MoveDown (C/Z) remain independent of head pitch.
The native controller combines and normalizes inputs before constraining XY;
it does not remap W/S into C/Z. Shift does not boost ladder speed in 2016.
See WALKTHROUGH-LADDER-INPUT.txt for the recovered control/pose/speed chain.
Structural collisions stop climbing; automatic hatch contacts still request
opening. Aligned adjacent hatches form a continuous vertical path.

EnterExit (default E) releases the ladder. Jump/BrakeOrJump (default Space)
also releases it. Geometry rebuilds invalidate attachment. Traveling beyond
the ladder span releases it; ordinary movement and gravity then resume.
The release jump uses .8 of the normal jump speed upward; the full original
pose-dependent jump trajectory and body animation remain outside this local
capsule controller. Capsule clearance and span endpoints are approximations
of the original point/eye path and ladder-well queries.

Validation
----------
Performed: read-only decompilation/disassembly, original/current PNG pixel
comparison, source review of shader/context ownership and movement branches,
and git diff --check. Compilation and runtime checks remain for the user.
Source comparison also verified that the sliced sampling body and cube-face
drawing body match the previous implementations, with commented-out code
preserved.
The worker refactor additionally passed a normalized comparison of the native
cube-face body, a check that paint preparation contains no sampling, face draw,
GPU completion or thread wait, and preservation of existing generator/shader
line comments. Threaded GL runtime behavior remains untested pending a build.

After the user's build:
  - Open the editor and immediately enter render mode, then repeat after
    spending time in the floor plan. Input should remain usable during sky
    preparation; a solid navy cube should change directly to the finished sky.
    No black or partially rendered faces should appear. Repeated mode
    switches should reuse the finished sky without regenerating it.
  - Change skybox size/mipmaps midway through preparation, disable/re-enable
    it, and close the editor while preparation is incomplete. Check that
    there are no partial cube faces, repeated brightening or stale GL objects.
  - Enter render mode with an empty design and a ship. Look around outside
    and through windows; sky orientation should follow look, not translation.
  - Toggle skybox settings, change its resolution/mipmaps and FOV, resize on
    a high-DPI display, switch floor plan/render, and close/reopen the editor.
    Check the ship, translucent windows, crosshair and sidebar still render.
  - Try each hatch orientation from above and below, at positive/negative
    decks. E should center the avatar and face the rungs once.
  - While attached, hold forward/strafe and look around: XY must stay fixed.
    W must climb up looking up and down looking down; S reverses direction.
    Level W adds no climbing force; C/Z work independently of camera pitch.
    Check combined controls and continuous forward through the native controller;
    Shift must not change climbing speed. Releasing controls stops movement.
  - Test stacked hatches, a closed automatic hatch, a closed manual hatch,
    and a nearby ladder behind a wall. No sideways push or wall teleport.
  - Press E and Space separately while partway up. Both must release; E
    pressed again may reattach and snap facing again. Rebound controls must
    work through the normal game binding table.
  - Pause in hand mode, open Settings, alt-tab, then return while attached.
    Release held movement cleanly; no drift. Edit/remove the hatch in floor
    plan and return: its old attachment must not survive the rebuild.
